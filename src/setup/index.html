To enable Handsfree, follow these simple steps to re-enable permissions in your browser:

  1. Click the Site Information / Tune icon (🔒 or 🎛️) on the left side of the browser's address bar.
  2. Switch both Camera and Microphone switches from "Block" to "Allow".
  3. Alternatively, open chrome://settings/content/siteDetails and set permissions to Allow.
  4. After allowing permissions, click Try Again below.
